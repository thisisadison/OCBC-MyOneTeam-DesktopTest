# OCBC MyOneTeam

An AI-driven wealth advisory prototype built around goal-based investing and life-event planning. Instead of a single generic robo-advisor, users are matched with a team of specialist AI agents plus an Orchestrator who reconciles their input into one coherent plan.

## Overview

MyOneTeam reimagines robo-advisory as a relationship, not a transaction. It combines:

- A personality-driven onboarding quiz ("Money Identity") instead of a generic risk questionnaire
- Named, explainable AI agents with individual reasoning, rather than one opaque model
- A continuous relationship — ongoing life-event simulation and quarterly reviews, not a one-time setup
- Clear hand-off points to a human Relationship Manager, so AI augments rather than replaces advice

## User Journey

1. **Financial Profile** — an MBTI-style "Money Identity" quiz resulting in one of 8 personas (Bubble Tea, Kopi O Kosong, Sugarcane, Soya Milk, Honey Lemon, Bandung, Barley Water, Milo Dinosaur), each with its own risk/planning profile.
2. **Build Your AI Team** — choose from 4 specialist agents based on your goals:
   - **Property Paige** — saving toward a home
   - **Retirement Ryan** — long-term retirement nest egg
   - **Growth Gary** — aggressive long-term growth
   - **Income Ivy** — regular passive income
3. **Goals & Risk Comfort** — set targets, timeline, and risk appetite per goal.
4. **Plan Building** — individual agent recommendations are reconciled by the Orchestrator into one blended portfolio (Fixed Income / Equities / Cash / Alternatives).
5. **Portfolio & Account Linking** — link external accounts (banks, CPF, fund providers) and fine-tune the suggested basket.
6. **Path to Action** — paper trade risk-free, send to a Relationship Manager for review, or trade live.
7. **Ongoing Relationship** — a MyOneTeam home screen, quarterly portfolio reviews, and a **Life Simulator** to model life events (buying a home, having a kid, career change) with the plan adjusting in real time.
